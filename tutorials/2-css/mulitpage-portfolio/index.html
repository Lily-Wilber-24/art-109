✦. ── About Me ── .✦

Lily Wilber is an multimedia artist that creates pieces the represent their experiences in the world. They explore these experiences using themes of danger, pain, love, distortion, and the human body. I want to connect with the people who identify with the gruesomeness that is depicted in some of my pieces and unsettle the people who do not understand.
