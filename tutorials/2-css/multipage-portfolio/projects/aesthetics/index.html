✦. ── Aesthetics ── .✦

This piece was made as an exploration of the aesthetics of the Remedy Entertainment game universe. When brainstorming how to best explore the aesthetics of such individual games I remembered that the games use full motion video in things like cutscenes and collectable videos. I thought using that same technique would be a great way to explore the worlds of the different games.
